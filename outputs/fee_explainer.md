# What the official sources say about free-plan limits and upgrades

- Free users have unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards, while file uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis and other tools have separate usage limits. [source 1]
- ChatGPT notifies users when they reach an applicable limit. [source 1]
- GPT usage follows the Free tier model limit; when it is reached, GPT access pauses until the limit resets, and reset timing is shown in-product. [source 1]
- Free users are limited to 3 file uploads per day, and OpenAI may lower upload limits during peak hours. [source 5]
- On the Free tier, advanced functionality such as data analysis, file uploads and image creation can have stricter limits than paid plans. [source 1]
- Go is listed at ₹399/month (inclusive of GST), and Plus at ₹1,999/month (inclusive of GST). [source 3]
