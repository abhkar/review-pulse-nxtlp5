# What the official sources say about free-plan limits and upgrades

- Free users have unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards. File uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis and other tools have separate usage limits, and ChatGPT notifies users when a limit is reached. [source 1]
- GPT usage follows the Free tier model limit. When it is reached, GPT access pauses until the limit resets, and ChatGPT shows the reset timing in-product. [source 1]
- On the Free tier, data analysis, file uploads and image creation can have stricter limits than paid plans. [source 1]
- If a Free tier account reaches a rate limit, usage rates reset when the account is upgraded to a Plus, Pro, or Business plan. [source 1]
- ChatGPT Plus is described as offering higher model limits, with more messages and broader model options than the Free plan. [source 2]
- Go is listed at ₹399 / month (inclusive of GST) and Plus at ₹1,999 / month (inclusive of GST). Go is ad supported and may include ads. [source 3]
