# What the official sources say about free-plan limits and upgrades

- The Free tier FAQ says everyday text chats are unlimited, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards. [source 1]
- File uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis, and other tools have separate usage limits on the Free tier, and ChatGPT notifies users when they reach an applicable limit. [source 1]
- GPT usage follows the Free tier model limit. When it is reached, GPT access pauses until the limit resets, and reset timing is shown in-product. [source 1]
- Advanced functionality such as data analysis, file uploads, and image creation can have stricter limits on the Free tier than on paid plans. [source 1]
- The page states that Free tier usage rates reset upon upgrading to a Plus, Pro, or Business plan. [source 1]
- ChatGPT Plus, priced at $20/month, is described as offering higher model limits, meaning more messages and broader model options than the Free plan. [source 2]
